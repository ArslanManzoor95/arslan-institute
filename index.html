About

Led by someone building with AI in the real world.

Arslan Manzoor is a data science manager leading AI implementation in a major pharmaceutical company, with experience turning emerging technologies into practical tools, workflows, and decision-support systems. He is also a qualified UK pharmacist, giving him first-hand experience of what it means to combine domain expertise with modern AI capability.

Arslan Institute was created to help students prepare for a world being reshaped by AI.

Academic achievement still matters, but future success will increasingly depend on something more: the ability to learn independently, think critically, communicate clearly, adapt quickly, and use powerful new technologies with judgement.

The students who thrive will be those who can question AI outputs, improve them, and use them to create useful things in the real world.

Our mission is to help students develop those skills before they leave education.

  • Real-world AI implementation experience
  • Data science and product thinking
  • Qualified UK pharmacist
  • Practical, hands-on teaching style
  • Focus on responsible and useful AI adoption

Why this matters

The question education leaders and parents are now facing.

Students are already using AI. What matters now is whether they will learn to use it well.

  • How do we move beyond blanket warnings about AI misuse and teach students responsible, productive AI use?
  • How do we help students use AI to think better, not think less?
  • What does future-readiness look like beyond grades, university applications, and traditional digital skills?
  • How do we prepare students for a world where AI is changing study, work, creativity, and every profession?

Arslan Institute gives students a practical, structured place to start.

Outcomes

What students walk away with.

Practical capability students can use immediately.

1

A smarter way to learn

Students learn how to use AI for revision, active recall, explanation, essay planning, feedback, and independent study without becoming dependent on it.

2

Using AI to sharpen their thinking

They practise briefing AI clearly, giving context, asking better questions, challenging weak answers, checking assumptions, and improving outputs through iteration.

3

The confidence to build powerful AI tools of their own

Students create an assistant, workflow, custom GPT-style tool, or prototype using modern AI systems, linked to a real academic, creative, or practical use case they can develop further after the workshop.

4

Responsible AI judgement

They learn where AI is useful, where it fails, how hallucinations happen, why verification matters, and how to use AI ethically in an academic environment.

5

Creative problem-solving confidence

The workshop helps students move from passive users to active builders: spotting problems, designing solutions, testing ideas, and improving them.

6

Debate, discussion, and presentation confidence

Students take part in detailed discussion about the benefits, limits, risks, and practical implications of AI use, challenge one another’s thinking, and explain what they built with clarity, confidence, and ownership.

We help students become capable users, critical thinkers, and confident builders.

Schools & Tuition Centres

Full-day AI fluency workshops for student groups.

A practical, interactive programme for schools and tuition centres that want students to move beyond basic AI awareness into responsible, useful, hands-on capability.

The same core workshop can be delivered in schools, sixth forms, and tuition centre settings. It is designed primarily for sixth form students, with adaptations available by exception depending on age, ability, and context.

Workshop format

  • Format In-person group workshop
  • Length Single module or full programme
  • Group size 10-12 students ideal
  • Audience Sixth form students primarily
  • Best fit Academically engaged, digitally curious students
  • Availability Online or shorter formats by arrangement

Flexible formats

Delivered as a single focused module or as the full programme, depending on your timetable, priorities, and group. The full programme is the complete experience; modules are a way to start.

Module 1

AI for Learning

Using AI to revise, recall, plan essays, and understand difficult material without becoming dependent on it. Students leave with a personal, repeatable way to study with AI.

A focused session of around 2 hours, adaptable to your timetable

Module 2

AI for Building

The hands-on build sprint. Students design and build a useful AI tool, assistant, or workflow tied to a real goal, and leave with something they made and can explain.

A focused session of around 2 hours, adaptable to your timetable

Flagship format

The Full Programme

Both modules delivered across a full day, the complete experience. Students learn, build, and present, moving from AI awareness to responsible, hands-on capability. This is the definitive version of the workshop; individual modules are focused entry points into it.

The workshop can be adapted for school priorities, student ability, available time, safeguarding requirements, and AI policy requirements.

Parents & Students

Personalised AI fluency sessions for students.

For parents who want their child to use AI as a genuine learning and building partner.

Private sessions are designed around the individual student. Some need help using AI to revise more effectively. Some want to understand how to use AI without becoming dependent on it. Others want to build tools, explore ideas, or develop confidence with the technologies shaping the future.

Session format

  • Format 1-to-1 personalised session
  • Audience Students of different ages, tailored by maturity and need
  • Delivery Online initially; in-person by arrangement
  • Length 90-120 minutes
  • Focus AI for learning, thinking, responsible use, creativity, and building
  • Outcome A personalised workflow, study approach, AI assistant, or project idea they can continue using

What a private session can cover

AI for studying and revision

Using AI for active recall, explanations, practice questions, planning, feedback, and understanding difficult concepts.

AI for better thinking

Learning how to ask better questions, challenge weak answers, check assumptions, and avoid simply accepting whatever AI says.

AI for building

Creating a simple tool, assistant, workflow, or project linked to the student’s interests, schoolwork, or personal goals.

Responsible AI habits

Understanding where AI helps, where it fails, how to verify outputs, and how to use it without outsourcing their own effort.

Confidence and direction

Helping the student see AI as something they can use with control and purpose, rather than something confusing or overwhelming.
