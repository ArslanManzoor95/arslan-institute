AI Fluency Workshop: Programme Detail

This brochure preserves the full programme detail behind the Arslan Institute workshop for schools and tuition centres. The landing page leads with outcomes and flexible formats; this page holds the complete agenda for planning, internal circulation, and print.

Full agenda

The complete full-day programme.

45 minutes

The shift

We begin with a clear, grounded explanation of how AI is changing learning, work, creativity, and professional capability. Students explore why domain expertise still matters, why judgement matters more rather than less, and why the strongest future candidates will be those who can think clearly while using new tools well.

75 minutes

AI as a learning partner

Students complete practical exercises using AI for explanation, revision, active recall, essay planning, research support, feedback, and learning difficult concepts. They see how AI can strengthen independent learning, improve the quality of revision, and help them engage more actively with challenging material.

60 minutes

Fluency foundations

We introduce the practical habits behind good AI use: context, prompting, iteration, examples, critique, verification, and responsible use. Students experience how quickly output quality improves when they become more precise, more demanding, and more thoughtful in how they work with AI.

2 hours

Build sprint

Students work individually or in small teams to design and build a useful AI-powered assistant, workflow, research tool, revision support system, subject-specific helper, or early-stage prototype. They learn how to define a problem well, scope an idea, make better implementation choices, test outputs, improve what they have built, and turn AI from something they consume into something they can direct and shape for themselves.

60 minutes

Showcase, debate, and reflection

Students present what they built, explain the problem it solves, justify the choices they made, reflect on limitations, and discuss how they would develop it further. The day closes with facilitated discussion around responsible use, future implications, and where human judgement still matters.

Institution benefits

What schools and tuition centres gain.

A credible AI fluency programme

Give students practical exposure to AI without needing to design an internal programme from scratch.

A balanced message

The workshop avoids both hype and fear. Students learn that AI is powerful, imperfect, and best used with judgement.

Visible student output

Students leave with something they built and can explain, making the learning visible to staff, parents, and peers.

A repeatable annual model

The workshop can run each year for new student cohorts, creating a practical AI fluency touchpoint across the calendar.

A timely offer for ambitious families

Parents are increasingly asking what skills students will need next. This gives schools and tuition centres a credible, practical answer.

A programme that complements academic support

The focus is not replacing tutoring or teaching. It helps students use AI to learn, think, plan, research, and build more effectively.
