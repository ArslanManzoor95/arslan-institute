The structure

Five stages. One working build.

What you see below is the standard session structure. The content of every stage is different each time, because the project your teenager builds is always chosen by them. We send a short questionnaire before every session so we can personalise each stage around their interests and starting point.

1
Discovery 10 minutes

We sit down with your teenager and figure out what they want to build. School subjects, hobbies, frustrations, ideas they've had. The output is a project concept that's genuinely theirs. It is not chosen from a menu. It comes from the conversation.

2
Foundations 15 minutes

A hands-on baseline in how to work with AI well. How to write a clear prompt, give context, iterate on the output, and use AI as a thinking partner rather than a search engine. Not theory. Doing. Everything in this stage connects directly to the project they are about to build.

3
Build 45 to 60 minutes

This is where we build the thing. We work at the right level for your teenager, from accessible no-code tools that feel like magic, to guided coding for those ready to go deeper. Your teenager is driving throughout. We are there to unblock, to push, and to make sure the session ends with something working.

4
Showcase 10 minutes

Your teenager walks you through what they made. This part matters. Not because it is performative, but because explaining something you built is how you know you actually understand it. You see them demonstrate something real, built by them, in their own words.

5
Next steps 10 minutes

They leave with a written guide to the tools and techniques used, and three project ideas personalised to their interests and ability. The point isn't dependency. It's capability. We want them to carry on without us.

Project examples

Real things. Built by them.

Every session ends with your teenager having built something they made themselves and can show you. The project is always chosen by them, shaped around what they actually care about. These are examples of what teenagers have built.

Academic

A custom study companion

An AI tutor trained on their A-level subjects, that knows their exam board, their textbook, and how they like to learn. More useful than any revision app they have tried before.

Creative

A creative partner

A writing collaborator, lyric generator, or story-builder tuned to their voice and the things they love. Something that feels like working with someone who actually understands their style.

Practical

A personal organiser

A revision planner, habit coach, or project manager built around how they actually live. Not a generic app. Their system, built by them, for them.

Ambitious

Their own AI assistant

A custom AI tool with a specific personality, knowledge base, and purpose that they design from scratch. This is for teenagers who want to understand how these systems work, not just use them.

Every session is shaped around what your teenager actually cares about. We send a short questionnaire in advance so we can tailor the build to them. No two sessions are the same.

Before we arrive

What happens before the session.

A short questionnaire is sent to you and your teenager a few days before. It takes about five minutes to fill in. We use it to understand their interests, their experience level, and any specific ideas they already have. When we arrive, we are already thinking about what to build.

  • A laptop or desktop computer with a browser
  • The short pre-session questionnaire, filled in
  • Roughly 90 minutes of uninterrupted time
  • A parent or guardian nearby for the showcase at the end
  • No prior experience needed
